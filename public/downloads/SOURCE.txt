WHOOP downloadable packs.

injury-management.zip
  BCCSA Injury Management resources for WHOOP-POL-022.
  Employer guide, employee PIM bulletin, sample forms, supplementary notes.
  Source: BC Construction Safety Alliance. https://www.bccsa.ca/

cor.zip
  BCCSA COR® audit tool, forms, policies, auditor guide, student audit assignment.
  Source: BC Construction Safety Alliance COR program.
  https://www.bccsa.ca/cor_program.php

BCCSA catalogues linked from the Safety Program (PDFs sit on BCCSA’s file server
and require a signed-in bccsa.ca session to download — they are not copied here
until a member download is dropped in):

  Heat Stress and Sun Safety
    https://www.bccsa.ca/resources.php?id_catalogue=84
    Heat Stress ECP, Managing Heat Stress at Work, Sun Safety ECP.
    Also useful: sun-safety poster; wildfire-smoke ECP under catalogue 57.

  Lone Hoist Operator Rescue Procedures
    https://www.bccsa.ca/resources.php?id_catalogue=98
    Construction hoist-car rescue templates and drill.

  Working at Heights
    https://www.bccsa.ca/resources.php?id_catalogue=46
    Fall protection rescue guide.

  Toolbox Talks (crane / rigging subset)
    https://www.bccsa.ca/resources.php?id_catalogue=1
    Chain and sling, crush/pinch, struck-by, electrical, heat, cold machines,
    loading/unloading, mounting/dismounting, communication, working alone,
    JHA, incident/near miss. Not silica, excavation, forklift, skid steer.
