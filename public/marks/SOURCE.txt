Regulator and standards marks used on this site to identify the bodies
the work is done to. They are not WHOOP marks and they are not a claim
that those organizations endorse WHOOP.

All marks are PNG with transparent backgrounds. Where a lockup uses
white-on-black or black-on-white type, a second file (`*-on-ink.png`)
is shown on dark paper and on the light-theme footer.
